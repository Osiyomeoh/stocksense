Move the medicine before the rain closes the road.

StockSense is a team of AI agents for the LGA pharmacist. It reads clinic stock, roads and the river through one Zetaris view, and drafts what to move or buy today. A pharmacist approves every line.

Eight agents, one plan

Code decides. The model only words it.

Seven agents are deterministic code that you can test. The eighth, the Coordinator, runs NVIDIA Nemotron to write the briefing, and every number in its text is checked against the plan.

Take one part away

How it is built

Three sponsors, each doing a real job.

Zetaris

One view joins four sources

The USL view clinic_risk is 38 lines of SQL. It joins sources that no single system holds.

  • Clinic stock, Postgres on RDS synthetic
  • Road register, spreadsheet synthetic
  • GloFAS river at Lokoja real 2022
  • Open-Meteo rainfall real 2022

The Evaluator re-queries Zetaris for every number a plan line rests on. A line it can't re-verify goes on HOLD.

Meterless

H-MEM remembers between runs

The pharmacist's supplier notes and the Forecaster's held road warnings live in H-MEM, with a trust ledger.

  • A note at confidence ≥ 0.70 changes the next plan
  • A contradicting note drops it to 0.60 and asks the pharmacist
  • The unmodified reference implementation, run as a small Node sidecar
NVIDIA

Nemotron writes the briefing

nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b, called through Nightshift, turns the verified plan into words.

  • Every number in its text must appear in the plan
  • If one doesn't, or Nightshift is down, a fixed template is used and the trace says why
  • Each recorded call keeps its compute receipt

Honest limits

What this is not, yet.

Synthetic clinicsStock, use, roads and closing levels are a synthetic Kafin LGA scenario. River and rain are real 2022 data.
A warning, not a dateThe August surge outruns any extrapolation from the week before. So a flagged road gets next-day transfers only.
A human approves everythingStockSense drafts. It never places an order or moves stock on its own.
Not a clinical toolIt plans logistics for one medicine, ACT for malaria. It gives no medical advice.
