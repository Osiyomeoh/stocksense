Watch the agents plan, step by step

These are real recorded runs of StockSense on the 2022 flood season at Lokoja, Kogi State. Every agent step is shown exactly as its trace file logged it. Nothing to install. Clinics, stock and roads are synthetic; the river readings are real GloFAS data.

Every number on this page is re-checked by one command, with no keys or network: python3 prove.py

Recorded in SAMPLE_MODE, which replays the Zetaris and Nightshift responses captured from live runs. The Nemotron briefing was generated by nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b through Nightshift.

River discharge: GloFAS via the Open-Meteo Flood API (CC BY 4.0), Copernicus Emergency Management Service. Kogi 2022 figures: WHO Africa, 2 Nov 2022.

StockSense drafts. A pharmacist approves, edits or rejects every line.
